01 — Disaster Recovery

Built for communities that can't wait to recover.

When natural or man-made disaster disrupts a community, JBW Federal is structured to respond fast, then get it right — clearing public property, restoring waterways, and securing the legal access that recovery depends on.

Every engagement runs on the same standard: experienced personnel, the right equipment, and a plan that holds up to federal, state, and local review — from FEMA debris guidance to USACE, U.S. Coast Guard, and EPA environmental requirements.

02 — Debris Removal & Environmental Cleanup

Public Property Debris Removal, executed end to end.

JBW Federal delivers comprehensive Public Property Debris Removal (PPDR) services in support of federal, state, and local disaster recovery efforts, enabling communities to swiftly and safely return to normalcy. Our PPDR services span every phase of debris operations:

  1. 01

    Assessment & Planning

    Rapid mobilization of qualified teams to conduct debris impact assessments, develop debris removal plans, and coordinate with local emergency management.

  2. 02

    Debris Collection & Removal

    Safe, effective clearance of debris from roadways, public facilities, parks, schools, and other publicly owned property.

  3. 03

    Segregation & Classification

    Identification and sorting of debris types — vegetative, C&D, hazardous, white goods — according to applicable FEMA and EPA guidelines.

  4. 04

    Staging & Reduction Operations

    Establishment and management of Temporary Debris Storage and Reduction Sites (TDSRS), with emphasis on environmental compliance and operational efficiency.

  5. 05

    Transportation & Final Disposal

    Hauling of processed debris to authorized landfills, recycling centers, or incineration facilities, with full traceability.

  6. 06

    Regulatory Compliance & Documentation

    Adherence to applicable federal, state, and local regulations, including FEMA guidance, and comprehensive documentation supporting reimbursement efforts.

Inland & Coastal Waterways

03 — Waterway Debris Removal

Restoring navigability, reducing flood risk.

JBW Federal provides specialized waterways debris removal to support disaster recovery and environmental restoration in rivers, streams, lakes, canals, and coastal zones — end-to-end solutions for submerged and surface debris removal from inland and coastal waterways, with a focus on safety, compliance, and environmental sensitivity.

  • 01

    Assessment & Surveying

    Hydrographic and aerial survey teams identify submerged, partially submerged, and floating debris.

  • 02

    Debris Extraction

    Safe removal of vegetative, construction, and demolition debris using specialized marine equipment.

  • 03

    Staging & Waterborne Logistics

    Water-based and shoreline staging areas for efficient sorting, reduction, and loading.

  • 04

    Transportation & Disposal

    Hauling waterborne debris to designated upland processing and disposal facilities.

  • 05

    Environmental Compliance

    Adherence to USACE, U.S. Coast Guard, EPA, FEMA, and NEPA considerations.

  • 06

    Reimbursement Documentation

    Detailed debris tracking, photographic records, and GPS mapping to support eligible cost recovery and FEMA reimbursement claims.

JBW personnel operate with full autonomy under the prime contractor model, with accountability held internally and aligned to government objectives throughout.

Right of Entry — Operating Model

04 — Right of Entry (ROE)

Access is part of recovery.

JBW Federal delivers comprehensive support in the execution of Private Property Debris Removal (PPDR) operations under a non-personal services contract structure — assisting in the collection of Right of Entry (ROE) documentation, the legal access required to reach private properties affected by disaster events.

  • 01

    Property Documentation

    Accurate, property-owner documentation across designated disaster-affected areas.

  • 02

    Community-Engaged Coordination

    A trained, community-engaged approach to gaining property owner trust and expediting documentation.

  • 03

    ROE Documentation & Data Management

    Trained field teams and real-time data management tools ensure secure, accurate, and timely collection of ROEs.

  • 04

    Government Integration & Reporting

    Detailed cost assumptions and clear work-element breakouts, enabling seamless integration with government expectations and oversight.
