01 — What the vehicle is

A direct buy between an agency and a certified 8(a) contractor.

The Small Business Administration (SBA) 8(a) Sole Source vehicle enables agencies to engage in direct buy contracts with certified contractors, streamlining and simplifying the acquisition process for Contracting Officers and federal agencies. This acquisition method provides for direct negotiations in clarifying scope and budget, allowing both the federal government and 8(a) contractor to reduce risk — and therefore cost — in delivery. It has the added benefit of providing a streamlined process for rapid acquisition, and is a recommended vehicle of choice for time-sensitive projects with complex (and sometimes incomplete) requirements.

  • 01

    Direct negotiation

    Scope and budget are clarified directly between the agency and JBW Federal, rather than through a competitive solicitation.

  • 02

02 — How an award is made

Five steps from requirement to award.

Each step below names who acts and what they produce, so a program manager or Contracting Officer can see exactly where a requirement sits in the process.

Sequence read top to bottom
Actor shown above each step

  1. 01

    Government Program Manager or interested party

    Define the requirement

    The Government Program Manager or interested party develops a statement of work, prepares government estimates and obtains the necessary funding.

  2. 02

    Government Program Manager

    Select JBW Federal

    Through direct discussions and negotiations with JBW, the Government Program Manager selects JBW to perform the work and submits a procurement request to the agency Contracting Officer.

  3. 03

    Agency Contracting Officer

    Offer Letter to the SBA

    The agency Contracting Officer contacts JBW's SBA Business Development Specialist, Mr. Sean Smith. Ultimately, the Contracting Officer will submit an "Offer Letter" to the SBA for the designated scope of services that JBW will perform.

  4. 04

    Small Business Administration

    SBA processing

    SBA processes the Offer Letter and returns it to the Contracting Officer who submits the Statement of Work and Request for Proposal to JBW.

  5. 05

    JBW Federal & awarding agency

    Proposal and award

    JBW submits the proposal which is evaluated and negotiated (if necessary) by the agency and the contract is awarded.
