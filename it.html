Information Technology

Technology built for missions that cannot absorb uncertainty.

Drafted — pending JBW review

JBW Federal's information technology practice supports federal systems across their full life: the framework that governs their risk, the engineering that integrates them, the operations that keep them available, and the analysis that reconstructs events when something goes wrong.

That work is organized into ten capability areas — from Risk Management Framework and Defensive Cyber Operations through Cloud Services, IT Service Management, and Salesforce Managed Services — delivered under the same federal requirements and accountability that govern every JBW engagement.

    Federal execution

    Technology isn't the deliverable. Mission continuity is.

    Drafted — pending JBW review

    Every JBW technology engagement is measured by whether the mission keeps running — not by the size of the stack behind it. The same expectations apply across all ten capability areas.

    • Security

      Systems built and operated with protection of data and access treated as a requirement, not a later phase.

    • Resilience

      Environments designed to degrade gracefully and recover in a known, documented way.

    • Integration

      New capability introduced into systems that already exist, without breaking what depends on them.

    • Continuity

      Operations planned around sustaining service through change, incident, and transition.

    • Accountability

      Defined processes, documented decisions, and a clear line of responsibility for the work.

    The five points above are drafted supporting copy, written from the capability names and JBW's federal positioning — no certifications, contracts, or performance claims have been introduced.
