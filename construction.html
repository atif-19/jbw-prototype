01 — Construction

Built for environments where execution matters.

JBW Federal's construction team works inside the compliance, security, and accountability requirements that come with federal work — not around them. That means staff carrying the security clearances sensitive federal projects require, a working knowledge of the federal laws and regulations governing the work, and construction methodologies chosen to control cost and protect delivery schedules.

That capability is organized into four areas: General Construction, Fuels Construction, Secure Construction, and the Operations & Maintenance that follows delivery.

02 — Capability Areas

Four disciplines, one construction team.

01

General Construction

Expertise
Decades of experience and expertise in federal construction, with a track record across projects of all sizes and complexities.
Compliance
A thorough understanding of the regulatory requirements and compliance standards governing federal construction, so work is performed in accordance with federal laws and regulations.
Security Clearance
Staff equipped with the necessary security clearances and certifications to work on sensitive federal projects, maintaining confidentiality and security at all times.
Technology
Current technologies and construction methodologies used to optimize efficiency, minimize cost, and enhance delivery — keeping clients informed and involved at every step.
02

Fuels Construction

JBW specializes in constructing and upgrading critical infrastructure that supports the transportation, storage, and distribution of fuels.

With a focus on safety, efficiency, and environmental responsibility, JBW helps build the infrastructure that powers the nation's economy — from fuel terminals and pipelines to storage tanks and distribution facilities.

  • Fuel Terminals
  • Pipelines
  • Storage Tanks
  • Distribution Facilities
03

Secure Construction

JBW is a trusted partner for secured construction — delivering projects in high-security environments where confidentiality, protection, and risk mitigation are paramount.

From government facilities and military installations to critical infrastructure and sensitive commercial properties, JBW understands the unique challenges of building in secured environments and delivers to the standard those environments demand.

  • Government Facilities
  • Military Installations
  • Critical Infrastructure
  • Sensitive Facilities
